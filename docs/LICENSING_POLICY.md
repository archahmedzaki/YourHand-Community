# YourHand licensing and contribution policy

**Project policy — owner adopted; not an independent legal opinion.**
The independently published community source tree is distributed under GNU Affero General Public License version 3 only (AGPL-3.0-only). This is the applicable public copyright license, not a noncommercial license and not a prohibition on competitors. Self-hosted copies may be used commercially under the AGPL's terms, and recipients retain all rights granted by the AGPL. The YourHand brand and marks are addressed separately from software copyright.

The maintainer plans dual licensing: an open-source community edition and a separately licensed hosted/proprietary edition. For source authored and owned by the maintainer, copyright ownership permits licensing the same material under more than one license. External contributions will be accepted only after a contributor signs an appropriate non-exclusive Contributor License Agreement (CLA) explicitly granting the maintainer the rights needed to distribute those contributions both under AGPL-3.0-only and under proprietary/commercial terms, without requiring publication of proprietary-only original work.

The CLA is a copyright license, not copyright assignment. Contributors continue to own their original contributions and may use them elsewhere; it does not transfer ownership of independent upstream code or third-party dependencies. A contributor who lacks authority (including employees whose employer owns the contribution) must obtain the rights-holder's authorized signature first. All code must pass provenance and privacy review.

**Hard limit:** Installing or copying community AGPL-covered code into a private production instance does not by itself make that code proprietary. The maintainer must own or separately license *every relevant copyright interest* in that combined code to distribute or offer the particular proprietary build without AGPL obligations. Third-party AGPL-only code, external pull requests without an executed CLA, and dependencies under incompatible terms cannot be automatically incorporated into a closed edition.

Track each accepted external contribution by PR, author, rights holder, CLA version/signature evidence, commit hashes, source provenance, and any inbound third-party license. Maintain separate open-source release and production inventories. Review the assembled build, not just the license headers of individual files, and honor AGPL obligations whenever offering the AGPL-covered build to users over a network.

A community contributor may submit a PR without signing immediately, but no contribution is merged into the official upstream until the maintainer verifies a valid CLA for its rights holders. No automatic CLA bypass for bots, corporate authors or small PRs.

Existing proprietary operational repository, customer enrollments, production configuration and private signing/authorization material are not granted under the community license merely because the public source tree exists.
