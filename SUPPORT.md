# Support YourHand

YourHand Community is open source. If you would like to support independent development, optional contributions will be accepted through the official PayPal link once the project owner verifies and publishes it. You may choose your own amount. There is no fixed donation amount and no donation is required to use the open-source software under its license.

**Donation link: not yet configured.** Do not send funds to URLs posted in issues, chat messages or unofficial forks. Verify any support link on the official project site and this repository after it is activated.

Donations are voluntary support, not tax-deductible charitable gifts unless explicitly and legally stated otherwise. They do not purchase priority access, support entitlements, ChatGPT tokens, or rights to customer devices.
